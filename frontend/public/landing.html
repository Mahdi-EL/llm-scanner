Process

How it works

From zero to full security report in three steps.

1
Connect your AI
Point LLM Scanner at your app endpoint, paste your system prompt, or use our built-in simulations. No code changes needed.
2
Run the scan
321+ adversarial prompts fire across 14 categories. Our 4-layer AI detection engine classifies every response in real time.
3
Get your report
PDF, HTML, Markdown and JSON reports with severity scores, proof-of-concept responses, and AI-generated remediation patches.
